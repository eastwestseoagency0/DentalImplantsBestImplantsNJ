Frequently Asked Questions

What are dental implants and how do they work?

Dental implants are small titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to serve as artificial roots for missing teeth. Once healed, custom crowns or bridges are attached to restore natural tooth function and appearance.

Am I a candidate for Same Day Dental Implants or Immediate Load Dental Implants?

Candidates for immediate load or same-day implants typically have sufficient healthy jawbone density and good overall oral health. A comprehensive dental implant consultation and 3D scan will determine if this option is ideal for you.

What is the difference between All-On-4 and All-On-6 Implants?

Both options replace a full arch of missing teeth. All-On-4 uses four titanium dental implants for support, while All-On-6 utilizes six implants. All-On-6 provides additional stability and is often recommended for patients with higher bite pressure or larger jaw structures.

What if I don't have enough jawbone for dental implants?

If bone loss has occurred, a bone graft dental implant procedure can rebuild the jawbone structure prior to implant placement, making almost every patient eligible for successful implant therapy.

How much do dental implants cost, and are affordable options available?

Costs vary based on the number of implants, needed prep work like bone grafts, and restoration type. At Best Implants NJ, we provide affordable dental implant solutions and customized payment plans to fit your budget.
